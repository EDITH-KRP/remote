Live Production-Ready Service Desk Platform

Enterprise Service Desk & Support Management

A highly-secured, decoupled, real-time client-server application engineered around ITIL best practices. Aligns seamless incident submission with dynamic auto-prioritization, auditing, and Socket.io communication.

Siloed Operations

The Corporate IT Service Bottlenecks

Arbitrary Incident Prioritization

Standard ticketing systems lack logical business matrices. Critical infrastructure outages are treated with the same urgency as minor font alignments, triggering SLA breeches.

Stale Data & Static REST Polling

Support staff rely on refreshing pages manually to discover new ticket submissions, resulting in long queue lag times, double-assignment conflicts, and delayed chat replies.

Non-Compliant Employee Profiling

Out-of-date directories lead to orphaned tickets without employee phone numbers or backup emails, making off-system follow-up during critical outages impossible.

SLA Impact: Traditional response delays reduce organizational productivity and violate standard operational compliance targets.
Modern IT Service Model

Unified ServiceNow Aligned Solution

Compliance Profile Guarding

Strict route guards block employees from raising tickets until corporate profiles (Employee ID, alternate contact, phone) are successfully updated and verified in SQLite/PostgreSQL.

ITIL Role-Based Dashboards

Distinct, tailor-made interfaces: Employees submit and audit their own tickets; ITIL Admins process queues with a visual drag-and-drop Kanban Board and support drawer.

Automated Priority Assessment

Computes incident severity dynamically upon submission via a 3x3 ITIL matrix of corporate Impact and emergency Urgency, ensuring deterministic SLA response tags.

Real-Time Collaborative Chats

Equipped with bi-directional Socket.io streams, staff and employees exchange work notes, upload file attachments, and audit reassignments without hitting refresh once.

Workflow Flows

Logical Service Desk Data Flows

A seamless sequence tracking database modifications and triggering automated real-time reactions on critical state adjustments.

Transactional Mail Trigger

Whenever a ticket is created or updated in the backend, Nodemailer initiates an asynchronous SMTP payload. It dispatches a formatted corporate confirmation to the employee's alternate email address to prevent service gaps.

Step 1

Incident Form Submission

Employee inputs category and describes details, attaching file objects via Multipart headers.

Step 2

Dynamic Severity Processing

The system evaluates urgency and impact ratings against the ITIL priority rules instantly.

Step 3

Sequelize ORM Persistence

A new SQLite/PostgreSQL ticket entry is saved along with structured audit logs tracking the changes.

Step 4

Websocket Broadcast Event

Socket.io emits a refresh broadcast (`tickets:update`). Connected ITIL drawers reload silently.
